Test yourself like it’s test day.

Four ways to prove you’re ready — a quick pop quiz, a basic test, a full computer-adaptive NCLEX mock, or a 100-question specialty deep dive. Every one ends with your score, your weak areas, and a rationale on every question.

How the adaptive NCLEX mock works

1Everyone starts at the passing line.

Your first question sits right at the standard the NCLEX uses to decide pass or fail.

2It adapts to you.

Get one right and the next is harder; miss one and it eases off — zeroing in on your true ability with each answer.

3It stops when it’s sure.

After 85 items it ends the moment it’s 95% confident you’re clearly above or below the line — up to a 150-item ceiling. Same rules as the real exam.
