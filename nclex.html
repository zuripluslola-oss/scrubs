Item type 2

Chart & trend.

Read the story across the vitals. NGN wants to know you can see it coming.

Trend item

Your post-op patient's vitals over 8 hours. What is your priority interpretation?

TimeHRBPTemp °CRRSpO₂
08:0088122/7837.01698%
12:00104108/6638.42295%
16:0012294/5439.12891%
Why:

Trending up in HR, RR, and temp while BP and SpO₂ fall is the classic deterioration / sepsis pattern (SIRS). The individual numbers matter less than the direction — recognize it early and escalate.
